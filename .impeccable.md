## Design Context

### Users
Personal expense tracking on a small phone first, with desktop as a secondary workspace. Capture receipts, check details, collect a draft, and close an expense report.

### Brand Personality
Warm, calm, orderly. The user confirmed retaining the current receipt-book style.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the cream paper, muted coral, and ink palette and existing typography. Use compact, readable layouts with clear primary actions and minimal decoration on phones.

### Design Principles
- Design for a 320px phone before expanding to desktop.
- Keep capture and review quick, with touch targets of at least 44px.
- Keep navigation within thumb reach and account for device safe areas.
- Preserve every receipt and report action across screen sizes.
